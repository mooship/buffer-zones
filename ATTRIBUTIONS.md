# Attributions

## Data

- **OpenStreetMap** — © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/). Gautrain, PRASA and A Re Yeng transit geometry is derived from OSM via the Overpass API.
- **Statistics South Africa** — Census 2011 sub-place boundaries (`SP_SA_2011`), mirrored by the community-maintained [SA-Maps](https://github.com/j-norwood-young/SA-Maps) repository.
- **City of Tshwane** — A Re Yeng route data from the municipal open data portal, where available.
- **OSRM** — drive-time matrices computed with the public [Project OSRM](https://project-osrm.org/) demo server.
- **Basemap tiles** — OpenStreetMap standard tile layer; satellite imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics.
